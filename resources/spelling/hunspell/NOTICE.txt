WriteLite ships LibreOffice Hunspell dictionaries for offline orthography.

- en_US.* — from LibreOffice/dictionaries (SCOWL-derived; see LibreOffice license notes)
- ru_RU.* — from LibreOffice/dictionaries (LGPL/MPL; see LibreOffice ru_RU README)

Engine: WeCantSpell.Hunspell (MIT)

These files are used only for local spelling. They are not explanatory dictionaries
and are separate from WriteLite lexical definition packs.
